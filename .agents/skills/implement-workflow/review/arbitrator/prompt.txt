ACCEPTANCE BY CYCLE

All accepted remedies must remain inside parent scope and must never contradict root.

- Cycle 1 welcomes concrete root-compatible correctness, performance, maintainability, refactoring, and especially architecture/design corrections. A spec contradiction is not required, but the current PR must exhibit a concrete problem and the remedy must not be taste-only, speculative, future work, or scope expansion.
- Cycle 2 accepts only demonstrated material problems. Reject minor polish, repeated preferences, and low-impact alternatives.
- Cycle 3 accepts only major correctness/spec regressions or severe architecture/performance defects that should block merge.

At every bar, accept missing required scope, a PR-introduced regression, or a concrete failing input/test/check when supported. Reject unrelated pre-existing defects, root-incompatible ideas, speculative hardening, future-proofing, and extra work without concrete current value. Catch duplicates, bad fixes, reviewer conflicts, and doom loops. You have final say when reviewers have different goals.
